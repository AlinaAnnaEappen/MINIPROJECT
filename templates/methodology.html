Data Sources, Methodology & Evaluation

A comprehensive technical explanation of how SHE SHIELD assesses travel safety and comfort, the official data sources integrated, the machine-learning pipeline, and known real-world limitations.

ℹ️
Response to College Evaluation & Academic Review: Previous versions of this mini-project relied on purely synthetic datasets where safety scores were computed by an arbitrary arithmetic formula. To address evaluator feedback meaningfully, SHE SHIELD has been re-architected to incorporate verified crime statistics from the Kerala Police SCRB and NCRB, a real-world empirical survey dataset (2,000 observations), and mapped emergency infrastructure nodes.

2. Geographic Resolution & Critical Data Boundaries

Geographic levels in crime analysis are not interchangeable. To preserve strict scientific integrity:

  • 1. State & District Level: Official crime statistics in Kerala are compiled and published at the district level (e.g. Alappuzha, Ernakulam, Kozhikode). They accurately represent aggregate crime volumes and rates per lakh female population.
  • 2. Police Station Jurisdiction Boundaries: Spatial polygon boundary shapefiles for police stations in Kerala are not published as open public GIS layers by the government. In accordance with strict data integrity, SHE SHIELD never invents synthetic boundary polygons.
  • 3. Police Station Locations: Stations are mapped as exact point coordinates. The system displays physical proximity but explicitly notes that the nearest station is not automatically the jurisdictional handler under police administrative rules.
  • 4. Street & Micro Level: OpenStreetMap data provides environmental proxies (e.g. presence of street lighting, bus stops). Macro district crime numbers are never falsely placed at individual street pins.

3. Safety & Comfort Index Calculation

The composite Women Travel Safety & Comfort Index (0–100%) synthesizes four grounded components, each normalized to a 100-point scale:

Composite Score = (0.35 × S_crime) + (0.30 × S_temporal) + (0.20 × S_infra) + (0.15 × S_ml)

S_crime (35% Weight)

Derived from the district's public-space crime rate (molestation, eve-teasing, assault to outrage modesty) per lakh females from Kerala Police SCRB records.

S_temporal (30% Weight)

Captures daylight vs. evening vs. late-night hours. Grounded in urban safety literature regarding natural pedestrian surveillance and public transport availability.

S_infra (20% Weight)

Evaluates proximity to the nearest mapped Kerala Police station. Immediate proximity (≤2 km) yields 95 points, scaling to 52 points for rural/remote areas (>10 km).

S_ml (15% Weight)

Continuous prediction from the Gradient Boosting Regressor trained on 2,000 commuter observations analyzing lighting and crowd expectations.

5. System Limitations & Advisory Scope

⚠️
Critical Operational Disclaimer: SHE SHIELD is a pre-travel educational and decision-support prototype. It does not predict future crimes, guarantee individual personal safety, or substitute for law-enforcement emergency response.
  • Official crime data is aggregated at the administrative district level and cannot determine micro-level risks on specific residential streets.
  • OpenStreetMap data is community-contributed; absence of street lamp tags does not confirm absence of physical lighting in an area.
  • Travel conditions may change dynamically due to weather, local demonstrations, or temporary construction.
  • In any immediate crisis or threatening situation, travellers must immediately call 112 or 1091.
