Problem Definition & Motivation

Why Proactive Travel Guidance Matters

Most existing women’s safety applications operate reactively — activating panic buttons or live location broadcasts only after an emergency unfolds. SHE SHIELD addresses the critical pre-travel question: “How suitable, well-supported, and safe is this destination at my planned hour of travel?”

⚡

Pre-Travel Decision Support

Evaluates potential hazards before departing. Users enter their destination, date, and planned arrival time to receive an intelligible Safety & Comfort Probability (0–100%) and practical guidance.

📊

Grounded in Genuine Crime Data

Incorporates published statistics from the Kerala Police State Crime Records Bureau (SCRB) and NCRB Crime in India reports, replacing arbitrary rules with empirical regional evidence.

🗺️

Interactive Geospatial Mapping

Visualizes destinations alongside confirmed police station locations, emergency telephone numbers, and 2km / 5km proximity rings to clearly depict available rapid-response coverage.

🤖

Honest Machine Learning

Trained on real public transit safety perception datasets (2,000 survey instances) using Gradient Boosting and Logistic Regression, with transparent precision, recall, and feature-importance metrics.

Analytical Framework

The Four Pillars of the SHE SHIELD Index

Our index integrates four verifiable factors rather than opaque, ungrounded heuristics.

PILLAR 1 (35% WEIGHT)

Regional Crime Pressure

Derived from official district-level police registries in Kerala. Examines public-space offense rates (molestation, assault, stalking) per lakh female population to capture baseline environmental security.

PILLAR 2 (30% WEIGHT)

Temporal & Lighting Context

Differentiates daytime travel from twilight, late-night, and predawn travel. Incorporates proven urban safety audit principles concerning pedestrian footfall and natural street surveillance ("eyes on the street").

PILLAR 3 (20% WEIGHT)

Emergency Proximity

Calculates exact Haversine distance to confirmed mapped police stations and emergency healthcare facilities. Provides direct telephonic contact to local station houses and Kerala Pink Police patrol teams.

PILLAR 4 (15% WEIGHT)

Transit Comfort ML Model

Synthesizes surveyed public transit perceptions (crowd density, lighting, reserved seats, driver verification) through an empirical Gradient Boosting model to reflect experiential comfort levels.

📜

Our Commitment to Data Integrity & Academic Honesty

Following academic review and feedback, SHE SHIELD intentionally avoids manufacturing synthetic crime coordinates or fictitious street-level crime counts. In India, verified crime records are published at the State and District levels. We explicitly present district-level crime statistics for what they are — macro administrative context — and display police stations as mapped infrastructure points with honest distance measurements.
