About the SHE SHIELD Project

A Sixth Semester Bachelor of Technology Mini Project developed at the Department of Computer Engineering, College of Engineering Chengannur, under APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala.

🏛️

College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC)

Department of Computer Engineering • Alappuzha, Kerala

The Women Travel Safety & Comfort Index and Advisory System (SHE SHIELD) was conceived to bridge the gap between reactive emergency alert tools and proactive pre-travel decision-support platforms. By combining geospatial analysis, official police statistics, and machine learning, the project enables women travellers to assess destination viability prior to commencement of travel.

Literature Review & Foundations

1. Safetipin Survey-Based Urban Audit Framework (2023)

Analyzed urban safety parameters including lighting, openness, security presence, and crowd density. SHE SHIELD builds upon these physical audit principles by integrating algorithmic pre-travel forecasting.

2. Next Gen Travel Safety for Women (IJCRT, 2025)

Examined GPS routing and transit verification. Identified key gaps in pre-travel risk calculation, which SHE SHIELD addresses through its multi-tiered scoring framework.

3. Women Safety App Research (IJRASET)

Emphasized user accessibility and emergency service connectivity. SHE SHIELD integrates direct station dialing and verified helpline linkages.
